    How to Play

    Be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

    The basics

    1. Everyone starts with 7 cards. One card is turned face up to start the discard pile.
    2. Match the top card by color or by number/symbol, then play it from your hand.
    3. No match? Draw one card from the deck and your turn ends.
    4. Last card: when you are down to one card, AYO! is called for you.

    Special cards

    • SkipThe next player loses their turn.
    • ReverseThe direction of play flips.
    • +2
      Draw TwoThe next player draws 2 cards, unless they stack a +2 or +4 on top.
    • Color ChangePlay it on any card, then choose the new color.
    • +4
      Wild +4Choose a color; the next player draws 4. You may only play it if you have no card of the current color.

    Stacking penalties

    When you are hit by a +2 or +4 you can pass it on by playing another +2 or +4. The penalty keeps adding up until someone cannot (or does not want to) stack and has to draw all the cards.

    Tips

    • Keep your Wild cards for later, they are your best way out of a tight spot.
    • Watch the card counts: a player's count turns red when they have 2 cards or fewer.
    • The gold ring on the table points at whose turn it is. The dial in the corner shows the direction.
